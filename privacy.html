Privacy policy

home-energy-backup is used solely by its owner, for personal use, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. That means it can only see and manage files that it created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.

How the data is used

The only data stored is the owner's own backup files. They are encrypted before they leave the owner's home, so neither Google nor anyone else can read them.

No data is collected from, or about, any other person. No data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. There are no analytics or tracking.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
